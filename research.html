Academic Profile

Dr Naveed Ejaz

Lecturer in Computing
School of Computing, Ulster University
Belfast, Northern Ireland, UK

My research focuses on applied artificial intelligence, with particular interests in multimodal AI, computer vision, natural language processing, responsible AI, and intelligent decision-making. My work explores the development of AI systems that can operate reliably in real-world environments, with an emphasis on practical deployment, uncertainty, privacy, and societal impact.
