Research

Research & Innovation

My research focuses on the development and application of artificial intelligence for real-world problems, with particular interests in multimodal AI, computer vision, natural language processing, responsible AI, and intelligent decision-making. I am particularly interested in AI systems that can operate reliably in complex, uncertain, and data-constrained environments.

01

Research Themes

Multimodal Artificial Intelligence

AI systems that integrate and reason across multiple modalities, including vision, language, video, and other forms of contextual information.

Computer Vision

Visual understanding, recognition, detection, and analysis for practical applications and challenging real-world environments.

Natural Language Processing & LLMs

Language-based AI, large language models, multimodal language understanding, and their application to intelligent systems.

Responsible & Trustworthy AI

Developing AI systems with greater emphasis on privacy, reliability, transparency, uncertainty, and responsible deployment.

Edge & Cloud AI

Distributed AI architectures that balance computational resources, latency, privacy, communication costs, and real-world deployment requirements.

Agentic AI

Intelligent AI systems capable of assessing situations, reasoning about uncertainty, selecting actions, and adapting their behaviour to changing conditions.

Privacy-Preserving AI

AI approaches that seek to protect sensitive information while retaining useful representations and predictive capabilities.

AI for Real-World Applications

Applied AI research addressing practical challenges in areas such as healthcare, infrastructure, environmental monitoring, and societal systems.

02

Current Research

Decision-Aware Agentic AI for Adaptive Monitoring of Lough Neagh and its Catchment Under Uncertainty

Research into decision-aware AI systems for adaptive environmental monitoring, with an emphasis on uncertainty, information acquisition, decision-making, and quantum-compatible optimisation.

Multimodal AI for Climate-Resilient Environmental Monitoring

Developing multimodal AI approaches for environmental monitoring in data-constrained regions, combining heterogeneous sources of information to support resilient decision-making.

Privacy-Aware Video Question Answering

Research on privacy-aware multimodal VideoQA systems, including privacy-preserving representations, semantic extraction, feature abstraction, and efficient edge-cloud processing.

03

Research Approach

My research is strongly application-oriented, with an emphasis on translating AI methods into systems that can address practical challenges. This includes working with multimodal data, imperfect or incomplete information, resource constraints, and uncertainty.

A particular interest is the development of AI systems that do not simply produce predictions, but can also assess the reliability of their predictions and support informed decisions about when to act, seek additional information, or abstain.
