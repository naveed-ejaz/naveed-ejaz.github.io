Supervision Approach

Research Development

Supporting students in defining research questions, reviewing the literature, identifying research gaps and developing a clear research methodology.

Technical Guidance

Providing guidance on machine learning, deep learning, computer vision, NLP, multimodal systems and the development of experimental AI solutions.

Evaluation & Reproducibility

Emphasising rigorous experimentation, appropriate evaluation metrics, reproducible research practices and critical analysis of experimental results.

Research Communication

Supporting students in communicating their research through academic writing, presentations, conference papers and journal publications where appropriate.
